Helpdesk & support

When something breaks, your staff click the OpenSprint icon on their computer, email us or send a message. Every request becomes a ticket, sorted by urgency and handled by a real engineer. There's no limit on how often they ask, and no meter running.

Included in both plans. 24/7 critical support on Complete.

  • Unlimited remote support
  • One-click help from your computer, plus email and chat
  • Screen sharing when it's quicker to show us
  • Clear response times on every plan
  • New starter setup before day one
  • Leaver access removed the same day
  • Help with printers, apps and the odd mystery

Cybersecurity

Most attacks on small businesses start with a stolen password or a convincing email. We put the controls in place that stop the common attacks, and we report against the Australian Government's Essential Eight so you can see where you stand.

Included in both plans, using the security tools built into Microsoft 365 Business Premium. 24/7 monitoring on Complete.

  • Endpoint protection on every device
  • MFA on every account
  • Operating system and app patching
  • Email filtering and phishing protection
  • Security awareness training
  • Essential Eight reporting
  • 24/7 threat monitoring and response
  • Help with cyber insurance questionnaires

Microsoft 365 & cloud

Microsoft 365 runs most small businesses, and it's often set up in a hurry. We configure it securely, keep it tidy and handle the admin, from licences to shared mailboxes to SharePoint permissions.

Business Premium licences are included in both plans.

  • User and licence management
  • Email, Teams and SharePoint admin
  • Secure sign-in and access policies
  • File sharing and permissions
  • Licence reviews so you don't overpay
  • Migrations from other systems (quoted)

Backup & recovery

Microsoft keeps its service running, but it doesn't promise to get back an email or file someone deleted months ago. We back up your Microsoft 365 data separately, watch every backup, and test restores so we know they'll work.

Included in both plans.

  • Email, OneDrive, SharePoint and Teams backup
  • Every backup checked
  • Regular restore tests
  • Data stored in Australia
  • Files stay safe in OneDrive if a device is lost

Devices, ready out of the box

Order a laptop through us and it's registered to your business before it ships. It goes straight to the staff member, at the office or at home. When they sign in with their work account, security settings, Teams, Office and your apps install automatically, and their Desktop and Documents sync to OneDrive.

Every device is managed from the cloud from then on. If one is lost or stolen, we lock and wipe it remotely, and nothing is lost because the files are in OneDrive.

Device management is included in both plans. Hardware is quoted separately.

  • Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android
  • New laptops shipped direct and set up on first sign-in
  • No imaging and no IT visit for new starters
  • Disk encryption and security settings applied automatically
  • Lost devices locked and wiped remotely
  • Existing devices enrolled during onboarding
  • Hardware advice, quotes and ordering
  • Office Wi-Fi and firewall management

IT strategy

Good IT is planned. We meet with you regularly to review what's working, what's due for replacement and what's coming, so spending is budgeted and nothing turns into an emergency.

Twice-yearly reviews on Business. Quarterly planning and vendor management on Complete.

  • Regular IT reviews
  • Hardware replacement schedule
  • 12-month roadmap and budget
  • Dealing with your other tech vendors
  • Advice before you buy new software
