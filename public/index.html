Questions

Common questions

What counts as a "user"?

Anyone at your business who needs their own Microsoft 365 account. Their licence and devices are included, so there's no separate charge per laptop or phone. Shared mailboxes and meeting-room accounts are free.

Is support really unlimited?

Yes. Ask as often as you need to, by email, chat or the help icon on your computer. Because we're paid the same each month, it's in our interest to fix the root cause rather than keep closing the same ticket.

Can we phone you?

You can, but you'll get voicemail. Every message becomes a ticket, just like an email, and urgent ones go straight to an engineer. We don't run a phone queue, so our engineers spend their time fixing problems instead of answering calls. Every request is tracked, and nobody waits on hold.

Do I have to talk to a salesperson?

No. Prices are published here, and you can request a quote online. We'll talk only if you ask us to, or if we need a detail to price your onboarding.

What isn't included?

Microsoft 365 Business Premium is included. Hardware, other software and larger projects such as office moves are charged separately. We quote these before starting, and you can buy hardware anywhere you like.
