Questions

Pricing questions

What counts as a "user"?

Anyone at your business who needs support and has their own Microsoft 365 or Google account. Their laptop, phone and tablet are included. Shared mailboxes and meeting-room accounts are free.

How do we get support?

Click the OpenSprint icon on your computer, email us or send a message. Every request becomes a ticket. We don't run a phone queue: calls go to voicemail and become tickets too, and urgent ones go straight to an engineer. On Complete, urgent tickets are handled 24/7.

How much is onboarding?

It depends on how many people and devices you have and what state things are in, so we quote it as a fixed, one-off fee after a free review. You'll know the exact amount before you sign anything.

What happens when we hire or let people go?

Your bill follows your headcount. Add someone and they're on next month's invoice. Remove someone and they come off it. Setting up starters and leavers is included.

What isn't included?

Hardware, software licences (like Microsoft 365 itself) and projects such as office moves or a new server. We quote these up front at a fixed price. You're welcome to buy hardware and licences elsewhere if you prefer.

Can we mix plans?

Everyone in your business is on the same plan. It keeps your security consistent, since one unprotected account is all an attacker needs.

Is there a minimum term?

No. All plans are month to month, and you can leave with 30 days' notice. We'll hand over your passwords, documentation and admin access when you go.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. You can move up a plan at any time, and move down at the start of any month.

Ready for a fixed quote?

Tell us about your business and we'll send a written quote within one business day. No sales call needed.
