Pricing questions

What counts as a "user"?

Anyone at your business who needs their own Microsoft 365 account. Their laptop, phone and tablet are included. Shared mailboxes and meeting-room accounts are free.

Is there a minimum?

Yes, 5 users. Our plans are built for businesses with roughly 5 to 100 staff. If you're smaller, we're happy to suggest someone who works on an hourly basis.

Why is Microsoft 365 included?

Business Premium includes the security tools we use to protect you, such as Defender, Intune and sign-in policies. Supplying it ourselves means every client gets the same protection, and you get one bill instead of two.

We already pay for Microsoft 365. What happens?

We move your licences to us when your current term ends. Until then, we take the licence cost off your monthly price, so you never pay for Microsoft 365 twice.

Is there a minimum term?

Our service is month to month, and you can leave with 30 days' notice. Microsoft 365 licences are the one exception: Microsoft sells them on 12-month terms. If you leave partway through, you can take the licences with you or pay out the rest of the term. We'll hand over your passwords, documentation and admin access either way.

How do we get support?

Click the OpenSprint icon on your computer, email us or send a message. Every request becomes a ticket. We don't run a phone queue: calls go to voicemail and become tickets too, and urgent ones go straight to an engineer. On Complete, urgent tickets are handled 24/7.

How much is onboarding?

It depends on how many people and devices you have and what state things are in, so we quote it as a fixed, one-off fee after a free review. You'll know the exact amount before you sign anything.

What happens when we hire or let people go?

Your bill follows your headcount. Add someone and they're on next month's invoice. When someone leaves, we reassign their licence to your next starter, or remove it at its renewal date. Setting up starters and leavers is included.

What isn't included?

Remote support is unlimited on both plans. Projects such as office moves or moving off a server, disaster recovery after a major incident, setting up a brand-new business, and on-site visits (beyond Complete's 2 hours a month) are charged separately, at our hourly rate or a fixed quote agreed before we start. Hardware and software other than Microsoft 365 are extra too, and you're welcome to buy hardware elsewhere.

Can we mix plans?

Everyone in your business is on the same plan. It keeps your security consistent, since one unprotected account is all an attacker needs.

Can prices change?

We review prices once a year and give you at least 30 days' notice of any change. If Microsoft raises its licence prices, we pass that change on at cost.

Ready for a fixed quote?

Tell us about your business and we'll send a written quote within one business day. No sales call needed.
