How it works

Switching IT providers, without the drama

Here's exactly what happens from the moment you ask for a quote. You can do most of it without a meeting, and your staff should hardly notice the change.

  1. 2 minutes

    Check the price and ask for a quote

    Use the pricing calculator to see your monthly cost, then fill in the quote form. Tell us roughly how many people you have and what you use today. If you'd rather talk it through, say so and we'll book a time to call you.

  2. Within 1 business day

    Free review and a written quote

    We'll send a short list of questions and, if you agree, take a read-only look at your Microsoft 365 security settings. You'll get a written quote with your monthly price, a fixed onboarding fee and anything urgent we noticed. You don't have to change providers to keep the review.

  3. Usually 2 to 4 weeks

    Onboarding

    Once you accept, we contact your current IT provider (if you have one) and handle the handover. Then we:

    • Document your systems, accounts and suppliers
    • Collect passwords and admin access into a secure vault you can see
    • Install our management and security tools on every device
    • Close the biggest security gaps first, starting with MFA and backups
    • Show your staff how to get help (it takes about five minutes)
  4. Ongoing

    Unlimited remote support, one bill

    From then on, your staff contact us whenever they need help. We patch, monitor and fix problems in the background, and you get one invoice a month based on your headcount. On Business and Complete plans, we'll also meet regularly to plan ahead.

Getting help

How your staff reach us

We don't run a phone queue. Every request becomes a ticket and is handled in order of urgency, so nothing gets lost, nobody waits on hold, and you can always see what's been done.

If a computer won't start or someone is locked out, they can email or message us from their phone, or leave a voicemail.

  • The OpenSprint icon: click it on your computer to raise a ticket. Your device details are attached automatically, so we can start straight away.
  • Email: send it to our support address and it becomes a ticket.
  • Chat: message us for quick questions.
  • Voicemail: leave a message and it becomes a ticket. Urgent messages go straight to an engineer.
  • Screen sharing: when it's quicker, we'll connect to your screen and fix it with you.

If you leave

Leaving is easy too

We'd rather keep you because you're happy than because leaving is hard. If you decide to go, give us 30 days' notice and we'll hand everything over properly.

  • All passwords and admin access returned
  • Full documentation of your systems
  • A handover call with your new provider
  • Our tools removed cleanly
  • No exit fees
