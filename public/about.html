About

IT support the way we'd want to buy it

Buying IT support is usually harder than it should be. Prices are hidden behind sales calls, contracts run for three years, and it's hard to tell what you're actually getting. OpenSprint was started to do it differently.

01 Prices you can see

Every plan and price is on our website. You can work out what you'll pay before you speak to anyone, and the quote will match.

02 No lock-in

Month to month, 30 days' notice, no exit fees. Your passwords, documentation and admin access belong to you.

03 Prevention pays

A flat fee means we earn more when you have fewer problems, so we spend our time preventing them.

04 Plain English

We explain what we're doing and why, without jargon, and we put recommendations in writing so you can decide in your own time.
